I am currently a graduate research scientist at the Johns Hopkins University, where I am completing my work on 
diagnostic, cross-species, biomarker discovery of neuropathic pain - a necessary step towards developing personalized 
care and model-based closed-loop neuromodulation therapies. This work is an extension of my doctoral thesis under the
guidance of *[Dr. Sridevi V. Sarma](https://www.bme.jhu.edu/people/faculty/sridevi-v-sarma/) and [Dr. Yun Guan](https://anesthesiology.hopkinsmedicine.org/faculty/yun-guan/),
where I developed an in-silico closed-loop peripheral nerve stimulation system to normalize pain processing in rats
with neuropathic pain. Prior to this, I worked at a neurotech software company, [Neurologic Solutions](https://www.neurologicsolutions.net/).
where I developed EpiScalp, an algorithm which provides a risk score of Epilepsy from a single routine EEG scan.

Research Interests
==================

My research is focused on learning hidden, controllable patterns from neural data, leveraging expertise in techniques 
from signal processing, machine learning, dynamical systems, and control theory. I am particularly interested in topics
with translatable applications that can help real patients.
